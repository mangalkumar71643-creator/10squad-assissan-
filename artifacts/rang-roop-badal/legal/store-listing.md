## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Get ready to break some bricks! Rang Roop Badal is a fast, addictive brick
breaker game with a drag-to-control paddle, colorful power-ups, and a full
progression system to keep you coming back.

HOW TO PLAY
- Drag anywhere on screen to move your paddle
- Bounce the ball into bricks to break them and score points
- Don't let the ball fall past your paddle — you only have 3 lives!
- Clear every brick to level up and keep the combo going

GAME MODES
- Classic Mode — balanced difficulty with steady progression
- Endless Mode — survive as long as you can as speed ramps up
- Challenge Mode — complete objective-based missions for bonus rewards

COLLECT UNIQUE BALLS
Unlock and choose between multiple balls, each with its own color and
animation style — Nova, Ember, Volt, Frost, and more.

PROGRESSION & REWARDS
- Earn coins and stars as you play
- Climb the rank ladder from Bronze all the way to Grandmaster
- Unlock achievements for combos, perfect hits, and high scores
- Claim daily rewards just for coming back

Simple to pick up, hard to put down — how far can you break?
