Privacy Policy

Rang Roop Badal (Brick Breaker Ball) — Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Rang Roop Badal (Brick Breaker Ball) mobile game ("the app") handles information. We built this game to be simple and self-contained, and that is reflected in how little data it touches.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device data with us or with any third party. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not make network requests to any server.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — score, currencies (coins/stars), unlocked balls, achievements, rank, and settings — is saved only in local storage on your own device, using your device's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is automatically removed if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app may request standard device permissions needed purely for local functionality, such as playing sound effects and background music, and haptic (vibration) feedback on button presses. These are not used to access or share any personal information.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no personal data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change (for example, if online features are added in the future), this page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships.
